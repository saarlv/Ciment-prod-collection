# Blocks Academy

[flag: academy]. If the rail has no **Academy** row (under **New app** and **Brain**), it isn't enabled for this account yet.

## What it is

- Short lessons on using Blocks, from what Blocks is to building agents, sharing apps and running the account. Every lesson has a video recorded in the product, its audio and its full text, all on one page, and many end with **Your move**: a short task to do in your own workspace.
- Lessons are grouped in tracks, starting with **Start Here**. Lessons not published yet show **Soon** and can't be opened yet.
- A lesson marked **Per account** teaches a feature that may not be switched on for every account.
- Lessons are in **English** and **עברית** (Hebrew): the language menu at the top right of the Academy (a globe with **EN** or **HE**) changes the language of the lessons, their audio and the Academy's own words. Product labels in lessons stay in English, as the product shows them. The choice is remembered in that browser.

## Using it

- **Academy** on the platform rail opens the Academy home, shown one of two ways, chosen with the **List** / **Campus** switch at the top. The choice is remembered in that browser.
- **List** (the default): your progress at the top over **Your build**, a small tower of blocks per chapter, its number and name under it (hover a block to see its lesson, click to open it); then the card that picks up where you left off (**Continue**), or starts with the first lesson you haven't finished; then the chapter you're in, certificates, and every track with its lessons.
- **Campus**: the whole page is a 3D world. A road runs past a building for every chapter (track), in order, each built from a block per lesson: solid, glossy blocks in the chapter's colour are lessons done, a see-through block is a lesson started, glass blocks are lessons to do, and faint frames are lessons not published yet. A floating marker shows the next lesson.
  - Hover a block to see its lesson (number, title, chapter, length, done or not); click it to open the lesson.
  - Click a building's base or its name to show that chapter's lessons in the panel on the right, under **Continue** / **Up next** (the lesson to pick up). The row of chapter numbers at the bottom flies to a chapter.
  - Drag to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom; the buttons at the bottom left zoom, turn the view and go back to where you are.
  - A computer or browser that can't show 3D gets the list instead.
- A lesson is one page: its video at the top, **Listen to this lesson** (the audio) beside it on a wide screen or under it on a phone, and the full text below, then **Your move** and the next lesson. The chapter's other lessons are listed beside it (under it on a phone). Playing the video pauses the audio, and the other way round.
- Under the video, **In this video** lists its chapters with their times; the one playing is highlighted, and clicking one jumps there and plays.
- Video and audio resume where you stopped, on any computer you're signed in on: the position is saved every few seconds while playing, on pause, and when you leave the lesson.
- A lesson counts as done (a check mark) after most of its video or audio, after reading the article to the end, or with **Mark as done** at the bottom of the lesson. Once it's done, that spot says **Lesson complete** and the next lesson is the main button.
- **Your move** has **Open New app**, and **Copy prompt** when the lesson suggests a prompt. Doing a mission uses builder credits like any other message to the chat (`billing/credits.md`).
- Progress belongs to you, not to the account: switching accounts keeps it. Nobody else sees it.

## On the phone

- In the Blocks mobile app, the Academy is the **Learn** tab, after **Apps** [flag: academy]. Progress is the same as on the web: a lesson started on the computer continues on the phone, and the other way round.
- **Learn** starts with **Your build**: a square for every lesson, one row per track in the track's colour: a lesson done fills its square, a lesson started fills half of it, and a lesson not published yet is faded. The phone doesn't show the 3D campus. Under the squares: how many lessons are done and how many minutes that adds up to. Then the **Continue** card and every track with its lessons; a lesson that isn't published yet shows **Soon**. Pull down to refresh.
- **English** / **עברית** at the top right switches the language on the phone, and the phone remembers it (separately from the web).
- On the phone, a lesson is one page you scroll: the video at the top, then **Listen to this lesson** (the audio), the full text, **Your move** and the next lesson, and at the bottom **In this chapter**, the chapter's other lessons with this one marked. Starting the video pauses the audio, and the other way round. The video, the audio and reading the text to the end each count toward finishing the lesson. Video plays in the phone's own player, with full screen and picture in picture. Audio keeps playing with the screen locked or in another app, with play and pause on the lock screen. Both resume where you stopped, on the phone or the web.
- **Your move** has **Open New app**, and **Copy prompt** when the lesson suggests a prompt; **Open New app** then opens **New app** with that prompt already filled in.
- Without a connection, **Learn** shows the lessons and progress it last loaded; progress made offline is sent the next time **Learn** loads online.
- On an older version of the app, **Play video** and **Play audio** open the lesson's video or audio in the browser, and that progress isn't saved. Updating the app from the App Store or Google Play brings the player in.
