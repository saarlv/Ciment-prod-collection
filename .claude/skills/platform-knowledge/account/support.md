# Contacting support

## How to reach the Blocks team

- **Avatar menu → Support** (the avatar at the bottom left). It opens a chat with the Blocks team.
- Email: support@blocks.diy.
- **Book a demo** (avatar menu) is for sales conversations. Technical help happens in the support chat.
- Support replies within working days; replies can take longer around weekends and holidays.

## When to send the user to support (rather than answering yourself)

- Refunds, billing corrections, invoices sent to another email, moving a subscription between accounts, and downgrades.
- Goodwill credits after a problem, and trial extensions. **Never promise these.**
- Changing the billing email.
- Copying an app to another account.
- Anything that looks like a platform fault, not an app problem. For example:
  - Ella stuck repeatedly
  - "Request failed" that doesn't go away
  - a schedule that keeps running after its trigger was deleted
  - lost data
  - login emails that never arrive after checking spam
- HIPAA, security, legal and compliance questions.
- Feature requests and "when will X ship?" questions.

## What to include (makes support much faster)

1. Which **app** and which **page** or workflow.
2. **What they did** and **what they expected**.
3. **What happened instead**, including any message shown.
4. A **screenshot or screen recording**.

Support asks for permission before opening or editing an app. Saying "you have my permission to look at the app" up front saves a round trip. When the Blocks team opens access to the account, admins see it under **Account → Security center → Member sessions**, in the **Blocks support access** section, and can revoke it (see `account/account-admin.md`).
