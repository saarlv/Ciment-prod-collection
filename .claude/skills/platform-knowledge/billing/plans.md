# Plans

**Never quote prices, discounts or exact allowances**; they change and differ by billing period and credit tier. Send account admins to **avatar menu → Upgrade** (the plans page), which shows current plans, prices and what each includes.

## How plans work

- An account is on one plan. The plans page has a **Yearly / Monthly** switch, with yearly discounted.
- Plans come in **credit tiers**: the same plan with more builder and usage credits, picked from the **Credits tier** selector on the plan card.
- There's a **Free** plan.
  - It includes a small, one-time allowance of builder and usage credits that **does not renew** monthly; there may also be a daily builder cap.
  - Once its usage credits are used up, the account's apps can't run their actions until a paid plan is chosen. Apps and data stay intact.
- Paid plans run from self-serve tiers up to sales-assisted **Business** and **Custom** plans. Those use **Contact us** and come with hands-on help. Requests beyond the listed plans go to support or sales.
- Billing is in **USD**. Banks may show a converted amount.
- Promotions are applied automatically to eligible plans. On monthly billing a promo covers the first month only; promos apply to upgrades, not retroactively.
- **Annual plans:** the plans page shows annual pricing _per month_, but an annual plan is **paid upfront for the whole year**. Make this clear when someone asks what they'll be charged.
- **Quarterly plans** (some custom plans): the plan card shows the price _per month_ with **Billed quarterly**; each charge covers three months, paid upfront.

## What a plan decides

- **Builder credits** (building with Ella) and **usage credits** (running apps) per month (`billing/credits.md`).
- **Team member seats** and **external user seats** (`sharing-and-access/users-and-seats.md`).
- Features that need an eligible plan. The app shows an **Upgrade** or "requires a plan upgrade" prompt where one is needed:
  - custom domain
  - custom SMTP email sender
  - custom integrations and app secrets
  - some individual integrations
  - HIPAA (custom plans)
  - the Blocks logo being removed from published apps
- **Not limited by plan:** the number of apps, the number of workflows, and table rows.

Don't tell a user which specific plan includes a feature. Locked features link to the plan that unlocks them, and the plans page lists what each includes.

## Trial

- New accounts may start with a free **trial** of a higher plan's features ("Business trial — N days remaining" on the Billing page).
- **The trial never charges automatically** and needs no cancelling.
- When it ends, a dialog offers **Continue on Free** or **View plans**. Apps and data are kept either way.
- Support can sometimes extend a trial. Don't promise it.

## Advanced builder options

The Engine/Model rows in Chat settings, Visual QA and "use your own Claude key" are rolled out per account, not sold by plan.
